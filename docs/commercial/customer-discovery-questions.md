# Customer Discovery Questions

## Questions For Compliance Leaders

- Which workflows are hardest to explain during audit or customer review?
- How do you currently prove what happened across an automated workflow?
- What evidence format is useful to your team?
- Where do logs fail to answer compliance questions?
- Which AI-assisted workflows are blocked by auditability concerns?

## Questions For Platform Engineering Leaders

- How do you propagate tenant and correlation context across services today?
- What identity provider and JWT patterns are required?
- Where do idempotency or replay issues show up in your workflows?
- Which systems would need to emit or consume evidence events?
- What deployment model would your team accept for a pilot?

## Questions For Operations Leaders

- Which document or payment workflows generate the most exceptions?
- How long does it take to investigate a workflow issue today?
- What would make a workflow status dashboard useful to your team?
- Which handoffs between teams create evidence gaps?
- What operational metrics would indicate pilot success?

## Questions For AI Governance Leaders

- Which AI-assisted workflows need stronger audit controls before production?
- How do you connect AI outputs to source documents and downstream actions?
- What evidence do reviewers need to approve AI-assisted operations?
- What model, prompt, or output details must be retained?
- How do you distinguish acceptable automation from workflows requiring review?

## Qualification Questions

- Is there a regulated or compliance-sensitive workflow with executive urgency?
- Is there a named buyer or sponsor?
- Can the customer provide representative workflow data or events?
- Is there an integration owner?
- Is auditability a budgeted priority?
- Can success be measured within 30 days?

## Disqualification Signals

- No regulated workflow or compliance pressure
- No access to platform or operations stakeholders
- Buyer only wants a generic dashboard
- Customer cannot provide sample data, events, or workflow context
- No budget owner
- No clear reason to act within the next quarter
