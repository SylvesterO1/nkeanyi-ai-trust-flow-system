# Ideal Customer Profile

## Best-Fit Company Profile

- Operates in a regulated or compliance-sensitive market
- Runs document-heavy workflows with downstream operational or financial actions
- Uses or plans to use AI for document processing, knowledge generation, support operations, risk review, or payment workflows
- Has multi-tenant systems or customer-specific audit requirements
- Needs to prove workflow outcomes to auditors, customers, regulators, or internal risk teams
- Has platform engineering capacity to integrate APIs and identity providers

Best-fit segments include fintech companies, banks, credit unions, insurance operations teams, healthcare administration teams, enterprise SaaS companies, government contractors, payment platforms, and document-heavy regulated operations.

## Poor-Fit Customer Profile

- No regulated workflow requirements
- No need for tenant-specific evidence or audit trails
- Single-user or purely internal workflow with low compliance risk
- No platform team or integration owner
- Buyer only wants generic analytics dashboards
- Organization is not yet using or evaluating AI-assisted operational workflows

## Buyer Personas

- Chief Compliance Officer
- VP of Risk Operations
- Head of Platform Engineering
- VP of Operations
- Chief Information Security Officer
- AI Governance Lead
- Product leader for payments, document operations, or regulated workflow automation

## User Personas

- Compliance analyst reviewing evidence packages
- Risk operations manager investigating workflow outcomes
- Platform engineer integrating APIs and identity controls
- Payment operations lead validating idempotency and replay safety
- Document operations lead reviewing extraction and downstream evidence
- Executive sponsor reviewing readiness and workflow status

## Trigger Events

- Launching AI-assisted document or payment workflows
- Preparing for SOC 2, ISO, regulatory, or customer audit review
- Experiencing a payment replay, duplicate request, or workflow traceability incident
- Selling regulated SaaS into enterprise customers that request evidence packages
- Expanding from AI pilots into production workflows
- Introducing tenant-specific compliance reporting
- Consolidating audit evidence across distributed systems
