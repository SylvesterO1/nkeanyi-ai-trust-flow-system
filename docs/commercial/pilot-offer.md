# Pilot Offer

## 30-Day Pilot Structure

The 30-day pilot proves whether NAITFS can package a customer-specific regulated workflow into traceable, tenant-scoped evidence that business, compliance, and platform teams can review.

## Pilot Scope

- Select one regulated workflow involving documents, AI-assisted knowledge generation, payments, or compliance evidence.
- Integrate one identity provider or test JWT source.
- Connect one source workflow or simulate one representative workflow.
- Produce correlation-level evidence summaries.
- Demonstrate dashboard-ready visibility for business stakeholders.
- Review pilot results with compliance, operations, and platform teams.

## Pilot Success Criteria

- A representative workflow can be traced by tenant and correlation ID.
- Evidence includes system participation and workflow status.
- Business users can understand readiness, evidence counts, and recent trust flows.
- Platform users can validate authentication, tenant headers, idempotency, and API behavior.
- Compliance users can identify whether the evidence package is useful for audit review.

## Customer Responsibilities

- Provide a pilot workflow owner.
- Provide sample documents, payment scenarios, or workflow events.
- Provide identity provider details or test tokens.
- Define retention and reporting expectations.
- Provide access to technical stakeholders for integration review.
- Participate in weekly pilot check-ins and final success review.

## NAITFS Responsibilities

- Configure the pilot workflow model.
- Support API, identity, and evidence integration.
- Provide demo dashboard and evidence summary walkthroughs.
- Document pilot assumptions, limitations, and next-step requirements.
- Produce a final pilot readout with findings and production recommendations.

## Pilot Pricing Hypothesis

- Paid pilot: $15,000 to $35,000 for 30 days
- Credited pilot option: 50 percent credited toward annual subscription if converted within 60 days
- Expanded pilot: custom pricing for multiple workflows, additional compliance reporting, or dedicated integration support

## After The Pilot

If the pilot succeeds, the next step is a production readiness phase covering deployment model, identity integration, event retention, evidence reporting, support model, security review, and subscription scope. If the pilot does not convert, the customer still receives a documented workflow evidence assessment and integration recommendations.
